I’m **Muhammad Hunzla**, an **Applied AI Engineer & Software Engineer** with **6+ years of experience** building AI-enabled applications, intelligent automation systems, API-driven workflows, CRM solutions, data integrations, and full-stack software for international clients.

I work at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, software engineering, automation, APIs, CRM systems, data, and business operations**.

- 🤝 **I’m interested in:** Open-source AI projects, agentic systems, scalable AI architectures, production AI workflows, and intelligent automation.
- 🌱 **I’m currently learning:** Agentic AI, advanced LLM workflows, RAG systems, AI orchestration, cloud architecture, and production-grade AI engineering.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Python, Generative AI, AI automation, REST APIs, webhooks, CRM automation, GoHighLevel, Salesforce, HubSpot, backend workflows, solution architecture, and full-stack engineering.
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** I’ve spent 6+ years across software engineering, automation, CRM systems, data workflows, and AI, so I usually think about AI as part of a complete business system, not just a model or chatbot.
